# Teaching and supervision

I teach artificial intelligence in health at the University of Montpellier and supervise researchers working at the intersection of rheumatology, epidemiology and health data science.

## Teaching

Through the University's Digital Health School, my teaching includes:

- **AI in digital health: concepts, tools and practice**, for medical students.
- **Applied AI in Health: concepts, tools and practice**, for master's students in the Faculty of Science.
- **An introduction to AI tools in medicine**, for advanced practice nursing students.

The courses cover core AI concepts, practical applications and critical evaluation of models, including bias, robustness and validation. I also contribute to the development of AI training and micro-certification in health.

[University of Montpellier Digital Health School](https://ecole-sante-numerique.edu.umontpellier.fr/)
